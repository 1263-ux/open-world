# Huyu interpretive pocket gardens: assets

Checked 2026-10-31. The courtyard furnishings and planting are interpretive design, not surveyed campus features. Furniture reuses the project's warm wood / teal leg vocabulary; low beds are traversable. Solid bench proxies are synchronously constructed and independently owned.

## Quaternius selected crown candidate

* Source: [Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html), [official itch.io listing](https://quaternius.itch.io/stylized-nature-megakit), Models by @Quaternius. Official free Standard download, accessed 2026-10-31 via its no-payment flow. Standard license file explicitly says 68 of 116 models; CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Attribution is not required, but preserved here and in `public/assets/environment/quaternius-license-standard.txt`.
* Archive SHA-256 `298f6732b872e4cf7b30e6e7abf9641c7f6dc6b326df37ac089533ed7e3d58c9`. Archive is not distributed.
* Runtime embeds original `glTF/CommonTree_5.bin` static positions, normals, UVs and indices in `HuyuCourtyards.ts`, preserving both branch/trunk and crown geometry with node transform. Bark uses a flat rough material rather than its original 3.9 MB diffuse and 4.4 MB normal maps. The original leaf texture is 158,796 bytes. Full tree 3,182 triangles (1,882 bark and 1,300 leaf). Original bounds x -1.91..1.76, y -.24..6.76, z -2.16..2.06 m. Original binary SHA-256 `0e617612d50e6bb2ca3c64338383f8938ce9e04d55b548ff11cec7a7b7d8f072`.
* Texture `Leaves_NormalTree_C.png` SHA-256 `998612bdf0b76a22f5f54d65cd7a457815d2842d89653fa4348a4ebb42933ad3`; license SHA-256 `120710e542c3ebaf83856c4eb55b4a1e680593b39302eba7fdc5d942dfe78c58`. Source archive is not distributed. Tree runtime texture loading has a synchronous visible low-poly fallback; trunk and collision exist before loading, and a late texture is discarded after the scene owner calls the returned dispose hook before teardown.
* Asset-only Blender inspection with `--disable-autoexec` successfully imported and rendered the original and extracted crown. This is not evidence of in-game appearance, placement, loading, performance or navigation safety. No scripts or shaders from the pack were executed.
